# Privacy Policy — My Notes

_Last updated: 22 October 2026_

My Notes ("the app") is built by Shankaravarshith ("I", "me"). This policy explains how the app handles your information.

## The short version

**Your data is yours.** The app has no servers, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Everything you create is stored on your phone. If you turn on Google Drive backup, a copy is saved in your own Google Drive, in a private folder that only the app can open. I never see, collect, sell or share your notes, financial entries or tasks.

## What the app stores, and where

The app saves the following in a private database on your phone:

- Personal notes (title, text, colour)
- Financial notes (amount, income/expense, category, subcategory, date, optional note) and any custom subcategories you create
- Tasks, agenda items and reminders (title, details, date, time, priority, completion status)
- App settings (text size, backup preferences, and the email address of the Google account you back up to)

Your phone's own device backup (iCloud Backup on iPhone, Google backup on Android) may also include this database if you have that turned on. Apple and Google handle those backups under their own privacy policies.

## Google Drive backup (optional)

Backup is off until you choose **Sign in with Google**, on first launch or in Settings. You can skip it and use the app fully without an account.

When you sign in:

- The app asks Google only for your basic profile (name and email address, shown in Settings) and access to the Drive **app data folder** (`drive.appdata`). This is a hidden folder in your own Google Drive that only this app can read or write. The app cannot see any of your other Drive files.
- The app uploads one backup file with the data listed above to that folder, either when you tap **Back up now** or, if automatic backup is on, a few seconds after you make a change. **Restore** downloads that file back to your phone.
- Data goes straight from your phone to Google over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. It never passes through any server of mine, and I have no access to your Google account or your backup.
- Google stores the backup under its [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Notifications

If you create a reminder, the app asks for permission to show notifications and set alarms. Reminders are scheduled locally on your phone, and no notification content is sent to any server.

## Deleting your data

- **On your phone:** delete individual items in the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything it stored.
- **Your backup:** sign out in Settings to stop backing up. To delete the backup file, open [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com), go to **Settings → Manage apps**, find My Notes and choose **Delete hidden app data**. You can also remove the app's access to your account at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and I collect no personal information from anyone.
