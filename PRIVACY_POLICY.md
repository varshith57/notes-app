# Privacy Policy — My Notes

_Last updated: [DATE]_

My Notes ("the app") is built by [YOUR NAME] ("I", "me"). This policy explains how the app handles your information.

## The short version

**Everything you create stays on your device.** The app has no account system, no servers, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. I never see, collect, sell or share your notes, financial entries or tasks.

## What the app stores, and where

The app saves the following in a private database on your phone:

- Personal notes (title, text, colour)
- Financial notes (amount, income/expense, category, subcategory, date, optional note) and any custom subcategories you create
- Tasks, agenda items and reminders (title, details, date, time, priority, completion status)

This data never leaves your device through the app. It may be included in your phone's own device backup (iCloud Backup on iPhone, Google backup on Android) if you have that turned on. Those backups are handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies.

## Notifications

If you create a reminder, the app asks for permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No notification content is sent to any server.

## Deleting your data

Delete individual items inside the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything it stored.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.
